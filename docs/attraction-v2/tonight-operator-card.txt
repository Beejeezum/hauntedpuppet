QUICKEST UNATTENDED SHOW
1. Open After dark from Output.
2. Open projector. Move that window onto the projector display and choose Full screen.
3. Check the complete figure fits your projection surface, including smoke and legs.
   Stagehand's Place ghosts & layers controls placement and corner adjustment.
4. Select Night watch. Prepare Effects if you want sound, then press Start Night watch.
5. Leave the laptop running and connected. Blackout stops both picture and sound.
   After Blackout, enable Effects again when you want sound back.

Night watch runs eyes, spider, then Wisp. The empty beats are deliberate; the next
scene arrives on its own. Sound is sparse dry movement, with no music or speech.
Tapping an individual scene takes manual control and repeats only that scene.
If Wisp cannot play, a dark pause leads to the remaining available scenes.

FOR LIVE HOSTING
Use Stagehand. Friendly stays friendly; Evil stays evil. Only Mixed can change
forms automatically. Host leaves the moments to you; Auto selects existing moments.
The labeled buttons queue actions through the painted transitions. Blackout is
always immediate. The current library is six native segments, not sixteen finished
performances; movement buttons glide the complete painted character.

TO CONTROL FROM YOUR PHONE
In Output, choose Pair a phone controller, then Enable phone remote. Use the
address and one-time code shown on a phone on the same trusted Wi-Fi. The laptop
makes all sound. Phone and projector stay silent. Disable remote revokes access.
Changing from Stagehand to After dark: close the old desk, wait five seconds,
then enable the phone on the new desk. Your paired phone can use that handoff.

TO MAKE WISP SAY A LINE
Open Make Wisp say something. First audition and explicitly choose a voice. Type
one short line and send it; Wisp says the exact words. Up to three lines can wait.
This currently plays voiceover with the appropriate painted form; it is not live
lip sync. Stop speech or Blackout clears the queue. The eight new voice auditions
still need your listening choice; none was silently installed as the final cast.

ONE-FILE BACKUP
After dark has Save Night watch · 2:04 MP4. It is one movie with the effects inside,
so you can play it offline and set your video player to Repeat. Its positions and
sequence are fixed; use the live desk for adjustable placement and scene choices.
This is the eyes/spider/Evil Wisp atmosphere loop, not the proposed spoken story.
Stop the live desk before playing the file so two separate shows do not overlap.
