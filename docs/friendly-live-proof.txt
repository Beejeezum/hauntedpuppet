Friendly painted Wisp live proof

Entry: /assets/wisp-tonight/live-puppet.html (published), or source/wisp-performance-reference/tonight-demo/live-puppet.html (source fixture).

This proof is separate from finished-film playback and the earlier microphone mouth study. It uses the approved painting as its body, iris and eyebrow texture. A built-in ImageGen skin plate is used only in the small facial regions behind the features. The generated body/exterior is ignored. Its exact prompt and asset hashes are in assets/wisp-live/.

Runtime: existing pinned MediaPipe 1.1.1 in a local Worker; existing pinned PixiJS 8.22.0; one connected painted surface with 6,305 vertices. Continuous mouth opening/rounding, eyelid aperture/independent winks, bounded iris gaze, asymmetric brows, small head lean/roll, jaw/cheek response, shoulder/elbow/wrist deformation, trailing core and crest, and object-space fog advection. Joint motion is integrated in small steps to avoid folded cells at gesture extremes. This is a custom front-view GPU proof, not a Cubism/VTube Studio model or complete all-angle/finger rig.

Operator check:
1. Open in a current Chrome or Safari browser and choose Start webcam puppet.
2. Allow the browser camera request, face it in reasonable light and choose Calibrate relaxed face.
3. Test a closed mouth, ah, oo, smile, separate winks, down/side gaze and one raised brow. Body and mist should keep moving while you talk or pause.
4. Start microphone separately if desired. Speaker output stays off until Hear my voice is selected. Use headphones during calibration. Mic-only mouth movement is an amplitude fallback, not phoneme inference.
5. Try Wave/Curious/Delighted, open silent rehearsal output, and test camera Stop and Blackout. Phone controls cannot start camera or microphone.

No camera/microphone input is recorded or uploaded. The camera preview stays private on the operator page. Output and paired previews use only the rendered Wisp. Blackout/pagehide stop inputs, pending grants are cancelled, and late-granted tracks are stopped. Face loss returns to a living idle. Graphics loss stops capture and blacks output; reload the proof to recover. Existing key-owning servers are not restarted.

Automated evidence:
- tests/friendly_live_pose.cjs: channel mapping, asymmetric brows/winks, stale-face idle and noninverted cells across extreme poses/body-energy settings.
- tests/friendly_live_voice.cjs: silent default, explicit output, denial/resume failure, pending Stop and repeated audio-context/track cleanup.
- tests/friendly_live_browser.cjs: actual GPU with synthetic worker/canvas camera and oscillator mic; permissions, no-face fallback, output pixels keep changing with operator RAF deliberately paused, Blackout and pagehide.
- Existing film/output/Puppet capture tests protect earlier modes.
- Actual local MediaPipe WASM inference was separately exercised with Google's public portrait fixture in work/test-friendly-real-model.cjs. This does not establish actual private webcam performance.
- work/soak-friendly-proof.cjs exercises one minute of synthetic facial/body acting and projection foreground changes. This is not an evening soak or physical-projector test.

Remaining acceptance: the operator's actual camera/microphone, mouth/eye likeness and timing, perceptual fluidity, real speaker monitoring and physical projector. No new Runway videos were generated for this proof.
