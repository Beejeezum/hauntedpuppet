# Haunted Puppet attraction excellence

## Goal and completion rule

Build Haunted Puppet into a polished contemporary interactive front-door attraction: a beautiful painted Wisp with coherent cute/evil identity, continuous articulated full-body and facial acting, seamless shape transformation, dimensional translucent materials and body-attached wisps; distinctive responsive voice and concise guest-aware personality; effortless laptop/phone puppeteering; reliable silent projection and recovery. Evaluate desktop animation, graphics and controller software when it demonstrates a material improvement. Preserve working voice/webcam/phone/projector/fallback paths and credentials, deploy verified improvements, and continue until visual, interaction, performance, operator rehearsal and physical-projection acceptance gates are actually met.

The goal remains **active and incomplete**. Software tests, generated films, a library installation or a successful deployment do not establish attraction-quality acceptance. Human approvals apply only to the exact thing reviewed. Never substitute a prerecorded loop for the required responsive live character, or a matching endpoint for a coherent morph.

This document is the current plan. Earlier experiments, sources, release checkpoints and rejection details are preserved in [the October20 history](wisp-excellence-history-2026-10-20.md). The durable working log is `work/wisp-excellence-status.md`.

## Acceptance gates — all remain required

Use the repeatable15-second visual reel: notice a guest, greet, listen, speak a short line, wave, turn, transform to evil and recover to friendly. Review full speed and slowed footage plus interruptions. Label synthetic speech rehearsals separately from genuine provider conversation. This reel is one evidence slice, not the whole attraction gate.

1. **Identity and dimensional construction.** Approved cute/scary hero designs remain one being: familiar crest, facial ancestry, eye color, connected core and readable hands. Complete front, three-quarter, side and rear acting must hold proportions, material and correct occlusion. Reject opaque toy heads, skeletal shoulder construction, collapsing joints, detached components and missing/extra anatomy. Existing opposite-side/rear views remain incomplete.

2. **Whole-body and facial acting.** Head, shoulders, elbows, wrists, fingers, torso, sides, crest and lower core have coherent overlapping motion with anticipation, weight and follow-through. Eyes/lids/brows/mouth act independently; gazes include down/side glances, blinks and attention shifts. A rigid image translated, scaled, twirled or filtered does not pass. Review full speed, slowed motion, at least three loop seams, every handoff and interruptions against original whole-character footage. Do not weaken v28's fuller acting to hide a seam.

3. **Apparitional material and framing.** Preserve painted depth, meaningful spatially varying translucency, readable facial features and body-attached evolving fog. Effects turn and deform with the spirit; no rectangular smoke overlay, fish curls, hard blue outline or generic image wobble. Keep a coherent lower core during normal acting. Every intended visible frame needs at least15% complete-effect reserve on all edges; request20% production breathing room when generating. Preserve originals and explicitly report threshold failures. Intentional empty-stage departures are evaluated separately.

4. **Transformation.** Cute and evil change shape as one coherent apparition, without doubled contours or incompatible whole-image fades. The return must remove teeth/round hands before widening to friendly eyes; v20's friendly-eyes-with-claws defect fails. Inspect all intermediate anatomy, material, poses and timing. Prove both directions, interruption and immediate friendly audience recovery. Endpoints, an intermediate still or an unexecuted timed-conditioning specification are not an animated solution.

5. **Voice and guest interaction.** Distinctive natural cartoon-friendly and frightening voices need human listening approval; robot-like modulation fails. Keep concise doorstep-aware turns, generally3–12words and one idea, for friends/family/costumed visitors and groups. Do not pretend to see costumes without operator-provided or actual validated input. Rehearse greeting, listening, interruption, silence, costume reactions, goodbye and return to living idle with genuine provider audio. Test native conversation, response timing, barge-in, voice kits/direct puppeteering, audible intelligibility and failure recovery. Acoustic mouth estimates are not phoneme-accurate proof.

6. **Operator and paired control.** Laptop is the sole microphone/audible owner. Phone/projector stay silent. Welcome/friendly safety, scary intensity ceiling, Stop/Blackout and recovery must be clear, immediate and reachable through drawers/short screens and mistaken actions. Rehearse laptop and a real paired phone; cancel queued, decoding and current incompatible scary work on a friendly reset. Preserve webcam and existing native voice/fallback paths. A scripted click test is not human usability acceptance.

7. **Show library and spatial performance.** After the core is convincing, curate roughly30polished performances and editable2–3minute ambient shows, mainly menacing/spooky with friendly concise Halloween interaction. Include genuine turns/back departures, curved depth approaches/retreats, poof entrances, intentional empty intervals, tricks, expressive gestures and carefully controlled scares. Depth must come from perspective, occlusion, acting and vapor drag, not scale alone. Empty black can connect finished disappearance/reformation films; it cannot disguise an accidental missing body. Do not count planned slots as finished animations or expand menus ahead of visual quality.

8. **Projection and sound at the house.** Rehearse actual laptop, phone, projector, speakers and mapped space. Check placement, black level, brightness, smoke clearance, readable expressions at street distance, apparent depth, intelligibility and audience safety. Review actual footage. Browser render recordings are not physical projection evidence; none has been supplied yet.

9. **Performance and reliability.** Measure stable target60Hz presentation on the intended setup while operator and projector run together; separate display cadence from authored video frames. Rehearse sustained evening use, repeated cues, real background/foreground changes, context loss, reconnect, delayed decode, interrupted voice, owner closure and Stop/Blackout. Keep bounded resource histories and cleanup, silent remotes, recoverable fullscreen failure and deterministic painted fallback. Distinguish mocked, genuine-provider, headless-browser and physical evidence.

10. **Tooling and release discipline.** Alternative engines/controller software must demonstrate a visible or performance benefit with this Wisp before migration. Preserve art and working controls while piloting. Do not relaunch the crashed Inochi test copy, download oversized models into scarce disk space, or replace paintings with procedural stand-ins. Use pinned dependencies and retain licenses/provenance. Keep credentials server-side; never expose values, restart a key-owning server casually or create new security-sensitive access without the required action-time approval. Deploy verified improvements through the existing Git workflow and test the hosted result.
