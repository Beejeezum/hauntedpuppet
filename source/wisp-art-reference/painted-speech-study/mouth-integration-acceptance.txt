MOUTH INTEGRATION — HUMAN REVIEW GATE

Human feedback: scary V6 mouth drifts and looks pasted on. Rejected.
Friendly voiced study is closer, but is not approved for production.

The mouth must belong to the original moving painted face. A soft mask alone
does not establish integration. Preserve the native cheeks, upper lip, jaw,
lighting, grain and translucent material; introduce oral artwork only inside
the opening. Do not replace the surrounding face with a generated face patch.

Required moving-footage checks before promotion:
1. Closed/rest mouth returns to the native face without a visible patch pop.
2. Mouth corners remain attached during head tilt, body drift and blinks.
3. Lip and jaw motion carries nearby native paint coherently; no sliding decal.
4. Oral depth/teeth stay behind the lips, without stretching or exposed edges.
5. Speech onset/closure is smooth, with no rectangular or radial mask outline.
6. Evaluate full-body footage AND face close-up at original speed, including
   the weakest contour frames. A still image or landmark test is insufficient.
7. Preserve original body/hands/fog and review timing with actual recorded
   speech. Acoustic estimates must not be described as phoneme-accurate.
8. Human approval of the new moving result is required; prior body-motion
   approval does not approve a replacement mouth.

No visual fix or runtime approval is established by this checklist.
