Neutral scary Wisp — connected face topology, source-only study

This is a new authored split-lip mesh on the neutral-reference painting, not another mouth patch on the rejected attack movie. The same source texture supplies both lips, cheeks and jaw. Opening reveals an intentionally unfinished dark cavity sampled from the native contact line. It has no authored teeth or tongue, eye rig, body movie, fog simulation or side-view art. Not a production puppet and not approved for the app.

The study currently supports moderate opening/narrowing/widening mechanics, not convincing phoneme shapes. In particular the displayed OO control remains an insufficient rounded speech shape; do not claim it is solved or speech-ready. A separate painted corrective is required. Enlarged face detail is limited by the original approximately85-pixel lip span, not magically created by a denser mesh.

Visual iteration found defects the initial geometry test missed: floating-point classification put some upper-lip vertices on the lower jaw, creating jagged holes. Classification now uses authored row identity. Strong narrowing pinched the eyes; explicitly bounded lip-relative topology and fixed iris patches keep speech independent. Several subsequent combinations folded triangles. The retained moderate controls have a positive minimum triangle-area ratio0.18706 across45 sampled combinations, not a guarantee for arbitrary future deformations. A stronger rounded shape is rejected; do not expand controls without authored correctives and visual review.
