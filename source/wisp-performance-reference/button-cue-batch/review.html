HAUNTED PUPPET · WORK IN REVIEW

Three little hauntings

One continuous 31-second rehearsal: a friendly wave, a failed scare, then a menacing reach. Each performance leaves an intentional empty stage before the next arrival.

Drafts for review. This is not the finished story or the automatic show library. The last chapter is a manual scare.

Sound starts only when you choose to play it. No camera or microphone access.

What to judge

  • Do the body, hands and fog feel like one painted being?
  • Do the planned departures and arrivals flow naturally?
  • Do the sound effects fit the action and feel balanced?

Known limitation: the scary source clipped some outer fog. Its edge was softened and the whole clip was fitted into a fixed, buffered frame. That treatment still needs visual approval; it does not restore missing source smoke.

All 735 frames decode at 24 fps. Both joins have exact-black frames and silent sound tails. These technical checks do not prove artistic quality or physical projection performance.
