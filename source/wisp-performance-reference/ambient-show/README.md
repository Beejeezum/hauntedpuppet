# Wisp ambient films and planned transitions

Presets are nonverbal little stories: Wisp notices passersby, waves, watches, plays tricks and haunts the space. Character-synchronous sound effects are encouraged. Speaking is a separate live-interaction mode, not canned dialogue embedded in ambient videos. No narration, spoken boo or generated background chatter.

`catalog.json` contains **32 planned production cards**, not finished assets. The first editorial sequence targets 145 seconds before bridges and final trimming. Source films have now been generated and reviewed as studies; the `productionStudies` records link exact candidate hashes and their approval limits. None of these records makes a planned card ready. The human endorsed the animation direction, not every unmade clip or seam.

## First four production priorities

1. Keep approved fuller friendly living motion as the anchor; capture its exact entry/exit pose, drift phase and full fog envelope.
2. Complete a menacing living anchor with the same body ancestry and continuous hand/core acting. Review the existing v35 before buying variants.
3. Author departures/reformations that genuinely reach empty black. This gives honest flexible holds and safe changes of story without forcing a visible cut between mismatched ghosts.
4. Make the friendly writing scene: hand-led stroke guides reveal designed, legible **Happy Halloween** lettering. The letters glow, breathe and dissolve with the spirit. All text and effects stay inside the reserved projection area. Do not ask the video generator to invent precise spelling.

## Every boundary is part of the animation

- A matching port name is only a production brief. It is not proof of a seamless join. Align position, scale, heading, hand pose, gaze, drift velocity, fog flow, illumination and intended sound tail.
- Review the outgoing and incoming films together at normal and slow speed. Record the exact two media hashes and review evidence; regenerating either side invalidates the join.
- Use a dedicated authored bridge when a visible join cannot match. Never crossfade whole ghosts into doubled bodies, reverse a trick to fake recovery, or freeze a living pose while waiting for decoding.
- An empty-stage route is allowed only after reviewed complete disappearance and before reviewed empty-start reformation, with at least eight verified empty source frames at both boundaries. A deliberate black hold of at least500ms is an editorial beat, not a substitute for making the body disappear properly.
- Preload and decode the next film before reaching the boundary. If it is unavailable, continue a reviewed living loop or remain at an authored empty-stage hold. Never jump to an unrelated first frame. The public opt-in showdesk now decodes before an editorial fade-through-black and supports Stop/Blackout/recovery. It does not yet execute this offline exact-media reviewed-cut/bridge plan, and is not a finished 2–3minute authored show.
- Check the closing boundary of the entire2–3minute show, not just internal cuts. Keep optional scares manual/armed; a kid-friendly reset cancels pending scary material. Blackout remains immediate even when a graceful exit would look nicer.
- Each sound cue needs an authored event time, level, tail and cancellation behavior. Only the laptop emits sound. No automatic maximum-volume stinger and no phone/projector playback. Do not make an SFX timeline until the corresponding film timing is locked.

`transition-plan.mjs` is an **offline editorial gate**, not a renderer: it refuses missing/unapproved films, dialogue, unreviewed joins, changed hashes, incomplete loop closures and automatic manual-only scares. It can select an explicitly reviewed bridge or verified empty-stage route. Synthetic planner tests do not establish real film quality or live playback.

Run its source-only checks with `node tests/ambient_show_plan.cjs` from the repository root. The two-film showdesk implements preloading, interruptions and layer controls. PR9 adds paired phone controls and laptop-owned local sound effects, with its final full-regression gate still running at this checkpoint. Exact-media bridge playback, authored sound timelines, the finished film library and physical acceptance remain required. Preserve existing working app paths until this pipeline has actual accepted media and transitions.

Additional atmospheric cards: standalone fog lettering and a bat flock dissolving the lettering. Generated lettering/bat studies exist, but their final visuals are rejected or pending; no finished atmospheric cue has been delivered.
