# Layered painted rig — generation provenance

All components were generated with the built-in image tool from our approved Wisp paintings. Original references are unchanged. Selected PNGs are copied without repainting or programmatic pixel edits. Geometry fields and atlas bounds are measured separately at runtime.

Preserve the ivory painted body pixels; change only background transparency and excess remote glow.
