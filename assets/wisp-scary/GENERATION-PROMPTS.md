# Scary Wisp source prompts

Built-in image generation was used throughout; no API key or fallback CLI was used. All prompts requested native transparent output. The approved cute `../wisp/wisp-assembled-transparent.png` was the first identity/material reference. Later edits reference `scary-concept.png` or its face-free `body.png` derivative.

## Concept

Create the scary alternative form of the exact friendly ghost reference. Preserve luminous ivory textured material, turquoise translucent edge light, curled flame crest and curled tail family resemblance. Transform him into a magnificent sinister haunted-mansion spirit: tall swept crest, long flowing wispy claw arms, expressive angular icy-blue eyes with black pupils, enormous shadow grin, formidable brow, billowing smoky body. Beautiful contemporary feature-animation painted render, dimensional shading and masterful silhouette. Front facing, isolated full body. Family-safe, no gore, skeleton, clothing, props, text, background, or flat-vector replacement.

## Face-free body edit

Remove only both eyes, brows, sockets and the black mouth, replacing those areas with continuous ivory painted skin. Preserve silhouette, crest, arms, claws, tail, blue edge glow, absolute position, scale and camera. No facial marks remain.

## Rig extraction edits

Four independent reference-based edits preserve painted material and native 1024 × 1536 canvas: body core without arms/lower tail, isolated viewer-left claw arm, isolated viewer-right claw arm, and isolated lower curled tail. Each prompt removes every unrelated component and requests a soft overlapping attachment stump. Source edits changed component scale/placement, so final registration is recorded explicitly in `asset-manifest.json`; these are not claimed to be pixel-identical cutouts.

## Mouth atlas

Exactly four equal columns and three rows, no grid/text. Only deep blue-black ghost mouths with ivory lip rims, turquoise highlights, pale cartoon teeth and purple tongue where needed. Reading order: sinister rest grin; pressed M/B/P; huge A; horizontal E; rounded O; puckered U; upper teeth/lower lip F/V; tongue to upper teeth L; narrow teeth S; rounded teeth CH/SH; tongue between teeth TH; enormous theatrical fanged roar. Consistent scale and transparent gutters, no bodies/eyes.

## Standard eye atlas

Exactly four equal columns and two rows, no grid/text. Only paired eyes/brows with blue irises, black pupils, ivory sclera/skin rims and smoky lids. Reading order: neutral sinister stare; curious raised brow; delighted; concerned narrowed; closed blink; half blink; look-left; look-right. Matching eye spacing, transparent outside features.

## Downward eye pair

Only two eyes and brows. Pupils clearly below center, irises touch lower lids and visible sclera above. Upper eyelids open; brows curious toward a shorter visitor. Same dimensional ivory/turquoise painted material, narrow feathered skin rim. No body/mouth/background/text.

## Menacing eye pair

Only two eyes/brows, far more dangerous than the mischievous reference. Heavy sharply angled ivory brow ridges, deeply sunken midnight blue-black sockets, extremely narrow angular white-blue glowing eyes, tiny cold icy pupils, cruel piercing stare with pointed outer corners. No large adorable round irises, no blood/gore. Same polished painted ivory/turquoise material and narrow feathered skin rim. No body, mouth, text or background.
